Terms & House Rules

1. Monthly Rent & Billing Schedule

Rent charges are generated on the 1st of each month and payable on or before the 5th. A 3-day grace period is provided. Failure to pay dues within the grace period may incur configured late charges.

2. Check-Out Notice & Deposit Settlement

A mandatory 30-day written notice must be submitted via the tenant portal prior to intended move-out date. Security deposit settlement and refunds are processed within 7 business days following room inspection and clearance of all utility dues.

3. Code of Conduct

Residents agree to respect quiet hours between 10:30 PM and 6:30 AM, preserve hygiene in common washrooms and dining zones, and refrain from substance use or smoking indoors.
