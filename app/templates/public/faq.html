Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before moving into StayNest PG.

What are the rent payment terms?

Rent is billed on the 1st of each calendar month and is due by the 5th. A 3-day grace period is provided. Payments can be completed online via UPI, net banking, or verified cash recording through the tenant portal.

What is the security deposit amount and refund policy?

The security deposit is typically equivalent to one month's rent. It is 100% refundable upon move-out following a 30-day prior written check-out notice, subject to room inspection.

Is food included in the monthly rent?

Yes! All monthly rent packages include wholesome North and South Indian meals: breakfast, lunch (packed tiffin available for office-goers), and dinner 7 days a week.

What documents are required for check-in?

A government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, Passport, or Driving License), employer/college ID card, and emergency contact details are verified upon admission in compliance with local regulations.

Can I invite visitors or family members?

Guests and family members are welcome in the designated visitor lounge on the ground floor between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM. Overnight stays are restricted to ensure resident privacy and security.
