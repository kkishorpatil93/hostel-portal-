About StayNest PG

StayNest PG was founded with a singular purpose: to modernize and elevate shared living accommodations across urban India. We believe that stepping out of home to build a career or pursue higher education should never mean compromising on hygiene, nutritious food, or peace of mind.

Located in prime Koramangala, Bengaluru, our premises feature high-speed optical fiber connectivity, ergonomic study setups, biometric security, and dedicated full-time housekeeping. Every meal is prepared freshly in an in-house commercial kitchen adhering to stringent hygiene practices.

Our Core Commitments

  • Zero Hidden Brokerage: Transparent rent and security deposit policies.
  • Prompt Grievance Redressal: Dedicated digital ticketing portal with SLA resolution tracking.
  • Strict Security: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric entry, and vigilant on-site staff.
  • Wholesome Dining: Balanced North & South Indian menus crafted with fresh ingredients.
