Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection & India DPDP Act Compliance

StayNest PG collects minimal necessary resident information including name, phone number, email address, emergency contact details, and government identification proofs solely for identity verification, safety, and regulatory compliance.

2. Security of Identification Proofs

Any uploaded identification documents (such as Aadhaar card, Passport, or Driving License) are stored in encrypted, isolated private server directories. They are never rendered publicly, indexed by search engines, or shared with third-party advertisers.

3. Resident Rights & Data Erasure

Upon completion of tenancy and settlement of all financial accounts, residents may request anonymization or erasure of optional personal profile data in accordance with applicable Indian digital privacy laws.
